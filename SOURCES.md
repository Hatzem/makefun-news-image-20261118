## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin gather in Shenzhen (Asia / diplomacy)
- U.S. President Donald Trump arrived in Shenzhen on Wednesday morning,
  greeted on the tarmac by Chinese President Xi Jinping; the two then met
  Russian President Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit reception, the first
  three-way handshake of the three leaders, ahead of a first-ever trilateral
  summit on Thursday.
- Xi and Putin held bilateral talks in Shenzhen on Wednesday, pledging stable
  ties and a multipolar order; Putin said he expected a "big potential
  benefit" from the trilateral. Agenda per Washington: nuclear arms control
  (after New START expired in February 2026), Iran and Ukraine.

## 2. Iran hits Kuwait and Bahrain (Middle East)
- Iran early on Wednesday launched strikes against Kuwait and Bahrain,
  saying it targeted U.S. drone and missile sites at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem
  Air Base, which hosts U.S. forces, after the United States struck Qeshm
  Island in the Strait of Hormuz. It was the first Iranian attack on Kuwait
  since September.
- Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian attacks for the third time in a week;
  Kuwait confirmed engaging drones but no hit was confirmed. Damage
  assessments were not published.

## 3. Peltola flips Alaska; Democrats take 56–44 (U.S. politics)
- Democrat Mary Peltola defeated two-term Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan in
  Alaska, the Associated Press projected on Tuesday night (18 Nov WIB
  morning); with 50.1% of first-choice votes to Sullivan's 45.8% she cleared
  50%, so no ranked-choice runoff was needed.
- It was the last U.S. Senate race of the 2026 midterms to be resolved and
  gave Democrats a 56–44 majority after flipping nine Republican-held seats.
  Peltola becomes the first Alaska Native to serve in the U.S. Senate.
  Certification is still pending and a Justice Department appeal remains open.

## 4. Israeli tanks cross Gaza's Yellow Line (Middle East)
- Israeli tanks advanced in central Gaza on Wednesday near the Nuseirat and
  Bureij refugee camps, crossing the Yellow Line — the boundary of the area
  Israel controls under the October 2025 ceasefire — for the first time
  since the ceasefire took effect, sending displaced families fleeing again.
- Israel's military said troops and Shin Bet personnel began a targeted
  operation to dismantle a tunnel west of the line that threatened its
  troops; the tunnel claim was not independently verified. Three
  Palestinians were reported killed in strikes that day. The U.S.-backed
  Board of Peace accused both Israel and Hamas of failing to meet their
  commitments to end hostilities.

## 5. EU proposes €45bn loan for Ukraine in 2027 (Europe)
- The European Commission proposed on Wednesday a new €45 billion loan to
  Ukraine for 2027 — up to €25 billion for military assistance and up to
  €20 billion in general budget support — covering about two-thirds of
  Ukraine's estimated €70 billion funding gap for next year.
- The loan is "limited recourse": Ukraine repays the principal only once it
  receives reparations from Russia. It now goes to the European Parliament
  and Council; von der Leyen said she was confident all 27 members would
  back it.
- Sources:

## 6. Cyclone Akvan bears down on India (weather / Asia)
- Cyclone Akvan in the Bay of Bengal intensified on Wednesday and was
  expected to become a very severe cyclonic storm, with landfall around
  Friday (20 Nov) on the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and
  Visakhapatnam, with winds up to 145 km/h.
- Andhra Pradesh, Odisha and Tamil Nadu braced for extremely heavy rain;
  schools were shut in coastal districts and the Odisha SSB recruitment
  exam was postponed. The storm was centred about 590 km from Chennai on
  Wednesday morning per the state disaster authority.
